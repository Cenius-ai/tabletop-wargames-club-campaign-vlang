Claiming rules

How the campaign map is kept honest.

  1. Any member may claim a territory that reads Undefended. It takes your faction colour instantly.
  2. A territory already held by another faction cannot be claimed — the tile shows who holds it.
  3. The organiser can reassign any territory to another faction, or clear it back to undefended, from the arbitration table below.
